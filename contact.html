    Frequently asked questions

    Before booking, most people ask…

    Q. How do international guests pay?

    Pay the full amount — we do not take a deposit. After you confirm on WhatsApp, we send bank-transfer or Wise details. There is no card checkout on this website.

    Q. Can I cancel?

    Cancel 7 or more days ahead for a full refund. If weather is unsuitable, we refund or reschedule at no extra charge.

    Q. Which tours do you offer?

    Day trips around Phi Phi, Maya Bay, Hong Island, Krabi 4 Islands, and sunset / plankton packages — plus daily car rental in Krabi.

    Q. Is hotel transfer included?

    Most tour packages include round-trip hotel transfers. Exact pickup time depends on your hotel and will be confirmed after booking.

    Q. Are park fees included?

    National park entrance fees are usually not included. Adult/child park fees are listed on each package page when applicable.

    Q. What if it rains?

    Itineraries may change with weather and sea conditions. If a trip cannot run safely, we help you reschedule or discuss options on WhatsApp.
